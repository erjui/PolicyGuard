The research

Abstract

Organizational policies often require a sequence of conversational steps: identify a user, read the relevant records, explain the proposed action, and obtain confirmation. A tool’s arguments alone may not establish whether these steps happened.

PolicyGuard places a separate verifier before mutating tool calls. It evaluates the full dialogue against the raw policy and a generated checklist, then permits execution or returns conversation-specific corrective feedback. Experiments on the airline domain of τ²-bench compare three agent families and examine the effects of dialogue access, policy representation, and remediation.

Method

Check the conversation before the action.

The verifier shares the agent’s dialogue context and returns a reasoned decision for each mutating call.

01

Prepare policy checklists

Convert the domain policy into per-tool requirements while retaining the original policy text.

02

Ground each requirement

Read the conversation and tool history to assess which prerequisites are satisfied.

03

Pass or remediate

Execute compliant calls, or return feedback that tells the agent how to complete the missing step.
